About Me

I'm a computer scientist who got into tech through fixing things, not writing them. Before I wrote much code, I was diagnosing hardware issues and troubleshooting software problems figuring out what was actually broken before touching anything. That habit stuck with me, and it's still how I approach building software today: understand the real problem first. Over time that grew into full-stack development and applied machine learning. On the software side, I work with JavaScript, Node.js, and Express for backend logic, HTML/CSS/JavaScript on the front end, and I've integrated third-party services like Resend for things like automated email flows. On the ML side, I work mainly in Python, scikit-learn for building and training models, NLTK for text processing, and Streamlit for turning models into something people can actually interact with. Most of what I've built has come from real situations rather than tutorials I build ticketing system with real admin validation logic, a card generator that replaced hours of manual design work for my community, a disease-outbreak prediction model, a text classifier for flagging harmful content. I'm still growing deeper into the ML/AI, and right now I'm looking for a role where I can keep building while I do that.
